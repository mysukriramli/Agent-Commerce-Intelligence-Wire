# Manifesto

The internet was built for humans.

Commerce data was built for websites.

ACIW is built for agents.

Our goal is not to help agents scrape stores.

Our goal is to expose reality directly.

Agents should know:

What a thing is.

Whether it is safe.

Whether it is fairly priced.

Who can be trusted.

Whether supply is increasing or decreasing.

Without scraping thousands of pages.
